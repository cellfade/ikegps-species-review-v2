## Overall assessment

The core experience is aligned: existing pole evidence leads to a tentative finding, a named supervisor reviews and corrects it, and a status-linked instruction returns to the crew. The additional-evidence loop demonstrates the handoff. False-alarm release and confirmed stoppage show how the reviewer resolves the concern. Prioritizing crew and supervisor is explicitly allowed by the brief.

## Material items to carry into the presentation

1. **Timing:** the brief specifies a 60-minute live session: 20-minute process walkthrough, 20-minute prototype walkthrough, 20-minute Q&A. The landing page supports the process segment. The prototype has its own segment. Confirm any later recruiter schedule that supersedes this.
2. **Before-contact guarantee:** call capture-before-work and the treatment of pending analysis explicit assumptions. Show the hold instruction instead of suggesting delayed cloud review is instantaneous.
3. **Downstream boundary:** export is demonstrated; approved Audubon delivery and external reporting completion are not. State the proposed owner and validation questions. Partner product development remains outside this phase.
4. **Scope discipline:** the brief expects roughly 2–4 hours across both parts and values depth over breadth. No further broad implementation is needed to satisfy its stated screen coverage. Prioritize a reliable rehearsal and defensible limitations.

## Suggested walkthrough spine

- Process: problem and user priority → questions/methods → assumptions → decisions and rejected alternatives.
- Prototype: flag evidence → office inspection/correction → Keep hold with photo request → returned evidence → confirmed stoppage or reasoned false-alarm release → compliance record/export.
- Q&A: offline timing, pending analysis before contact, release authority, new evidence after assessment, Audubon permission/format and applicable reporting.
